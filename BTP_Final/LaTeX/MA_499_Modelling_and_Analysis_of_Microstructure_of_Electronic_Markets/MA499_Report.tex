\begin{center}
{\Large{\bf{ABSTRACT}}}
\end{center}


The primary aim of this project is to understand the functioning of elec-
tronic exchanges, the driving principles behind the dynamics of such markets,
how such dynamics affect the underlying asset prices and and the simulation of
such exchanges through models described in literature. The project starts by discussing the need for electronic exchanges and their subsequent advent. Thereafter, the discussion continues on the dynamics of electronic exchanges, primarily the dynamics behind their Order Books. In the first part, we provide the dynamics of the Order Book from the perspective of microstructures of markets. The second part of this project discusses simulation of Limit Order Books and some of their important features through the use of zero intelligence models such as Poisson processes and Hawkes processes, followed by deep learning approaches for the same using GANs and CNN-LSTMs.
